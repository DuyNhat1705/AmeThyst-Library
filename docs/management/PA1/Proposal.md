## 1. Introduction
Our project is a comprehensive library management system designed to modernize the academic experience. Its core value lies in simplifying online book reserving and strictly in-person borrowing, returning, and pickup processes for physical books, along with study room reservations.

## 2. Target Users and Environments
**Target Users:**
* Users who want to borrow books.
* People looking to form study groups or find study partners.
* Individuals seeking a quiet space to focus on their work or studies.

**Environments:**
* Web application.
* Compatible with all operating systems.
* Accessible via any device with internet access.

## 3. Key Features
### 3.1. Authentication
Ensures secure system access through role-based authentication (user, librarian, admin). It protects sensitive personal data while tracking account activity to maintain a safe and well-managed library environment.

### 3.2. Profile management 
Allows users to manage their personal information and track their current library activities. Users can conveniently view their active book reservations, borrowing history, and upcoming study room bookings in one centralized dashboard.

### 3.3. Borrow & Reserving Feature
Enables users to check real-time availability and digitally reserve physical books and study rooms for in-person use. This guarantees resource availability upon arrival, saving users time and preventing peak-hour waiting lines.

### 3.4. Searching Feature
Helps users quickly locate physical materials through an advanced search engine. By utilizing dynamic filters such as keywords, categories, authors, and real-time availability status, users can instantly find exactly what they need.

### 3.5. Libarian Administration 
Empowers librarians to efficiently oversee daily operations, track physical inventory, and process in-person book pickups and returns. They can also provide timely support to user requests.

### 3.6. Admin Administration
Provides a comprehensive dashboard with visual statistics on system usage, popular book trends, and peak study room hours. This data-driven approach allows administrators to optimize resource allocation and perform system maintenance.

### 3.7. Study Groups
Fosters a collaborative community by allowing users to connect based on shared academic interests or specific topics. Users can form groups, send join requests, and seamlessly book study rooms together for collaborative work.

### 3.8. User Assistance
Enhances user satisfaction by offering accessible online guidelines, and a direct support channel for troubleshooting. It also provides practical resources like library floor plans and policies to ensure a seamless physical visit.

## 4. AI Feature
### AI-Powered Book Recommendations
The AI suggests books based on the user's reading and borrowing history. By analyzing past interactions, the system recommends new titles that align with the user's preferences. This provides real, practical value by significantly enhancing the experience for avid readers who want to discover new genres or books but are unsure where to start. Ultimately, this delivers a fresh and personalized experience, increasing user engagement and retaining them longer.
* Input: The user's past borrowing history and reading interactions.
* Output: Curated suggestions of books the user is highly likely to enjoy.
* Value: Reduces information overload within a massive library database, helping users easily navigate options and personalizing their learning journey.
